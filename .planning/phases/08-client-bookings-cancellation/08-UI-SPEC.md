# Phase 8 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
>
> **Scope.** Requirements BOOK-05 to BOOK-09. Six surfaces:
> 1. My Bookings (replaces the `ClientBookingsRoot` placeholder): Upcoming and Past tabs, cards, states.
> 2. Upcoming Booking Details (new route `UpcomingBookingKey`), including the join card placeholder and the Cancel entry.
> 3. The shared `RescheduleResponseCard` (D-01 to D-04) with its accept and decline dialogs and recovery dialogs. It appears on Upcoming Booking Details and on Booking Summary.
> 4. Cancellation (new route `CancelBookingKey`): reason step, confirm step, refund preview, result.
> 5. Booking Summary (new route `BookingSummaryKey`).
> 6. The Today's Sessions card tap on Explore (hand-off to surface 2).
>
> Out of scope and must not appear in the UI: Reschedule action or picker (DEF-1), live Join and the join window (Phase 9), Message Expert and chat (Phase 12), Leave a Review and the Feedback block (Phase 13), push deep links (Phase 13), the private note screen, "My Note" card and "Private Note" buttons (D-16), paging past page 1 (D-09).
>
> **Baseline.** `01-UI-SPEC.md` (tokens, shapes, motion, shell), `05-UI-SPEC.md`, `06-UI-SPEC.md` (booking form, Request Sent, status pill, error dialog) and `07-UI-SPEC.md` (Payment Methods, `PaymentLifecycleCard`, destructive color rule A5) are locked and not restated. Dark only, Material 3, Nunito 400 / 600, exactly four text sizes (12 / 16 / 20 / 28 sp), `Spacing` xs..xxxl, `ClickMeShapes`, `MaterialTheme.extras`. Every value below is an existing token unless marked "(new)".
>
> **Design source.** Google Stitch is not accessible. The stand-in is `ios-reference/ClickMe2026/Views/Screens/Client Mode/{My Bookings, Upcoming Booking Details, Cancellation, Booking Summary}/**`. Copy is taken from iOS verbatim unless marked "(Android)". The per-screen palettes (`MyBookingsBrand`, `UpcomingBookingBrand`, `CancellationBrand`, `BookingSummaryBrand`) collapse to the Phase 1 tokens (FND-02); iOS hard-coded orange, red and glow shadows become `tertiary`, `error` and no glow. Layout is ported, not pixel-matched.
>
> **Source of decisions.** CONTEXT D-01..D-16 for everything listed there. This spec fills the "Claude's Discretion" areas: card and screen layout, tab control, skeletons, badge colors, dialog copy, error copy, snackbar copy and the date format of the proposal. The session had no live questions, so every assumption is recorded in "Assumptions and Open Items".

## Spacing Scale

Phase 1 tokens, all multiples of 4: xs 4, sm 8, md 16, lg 24, xl 32, xxl 48, xxxl 64.

| Token | Used for in this phase |
|-------|------------------------|
| xs (4dp) | Gap between a card's name line and its sub-line; gap between a section label and its value; icon-to-text gap inside the badge and payment pill; badge vertical padding |
| sm (8dp) | Gap between icon and text in info rows; gap between a card's rows; badge horizontal padding; gap between stacked dialog fields |
| md (16dp) | Screen horizontal padding (iOS 20); gap between cards in every list and column (iOS 14 to 16); card inner padding (iOS 16 to 18); reason row horizontal padding; gap between the two reschedule buttons; gap between avatar and text |
| lg (24dp) | Top padding under the tab row and under the app bar on the Cancel screen; section gap on the Cancel reason step (iOS 28); inner padding of the Confirm card (iOS 24); gap between the confirm card and its buttons |
| xl (32dp) | Bottom scroll padding on every screen (iOS 40 to 48) plus the navigation-bar inset; empty-state icon-to-text spacing (inherited) |
| xxl (48dp) | Minimum touch target (tabs, icon buttons, reason rows are 56) |
| xxxl (64dp) | Not used |

Exceptions (component dimensions and platform minimums, not layout spacing):
- Touch targets stay at least 48dp: tabs (`heightIn(min = 48.dp)`), the copy-link `IconButton` 48 x 48 (iOS 36), the cards (whole card is the target, taller than 48), dialog buttons, Retry.
- Controls (Join Call, Cancel Booking, Accept, Decline, Continue, Keep Booking, Confirm Cancellation, Book Again, Explore Experts) use `heightIn(min = 56.dp)` (inherited; iOS 42 to 56).
- Reason rows: `heightIn(min = 56.dp)` each.
- Avatars: 56dp on both My Bookings card types (iOS 60), 72dp on Upcoming Details (iOS 72), 64dp on Booking Summary (iOS 62), 48dp on the Cancel summary card (iOS 52), and 40dp is not used. Initials fallback is the `Avatar` default.
- Status dot in the status pill: 8dp. Tab indicator: 3dp (`PrimaryTabRow` indicator height). Warning circle on the Confirm card: 64dp, icon 32dp. Info-row icons 20dp (matches the Today's Sessions card), pill and banner icons 16dp or 24dp as stated.
- Comment field: `heightIn(min = 112.dp)` (iOS 110).
- Stroke widths 1dp resting, 2dp focused (hairlines).
- Skeleton shapes copy the real component dimensions above.

## Typography

Inherited: exactly four sizes (12, 16, 20, 28 sp), two weights (400, 600), line height 1.5 body and 1.2 headings. Nothing in this phase sets `fontSize`; every `Text` takes an M3 slot. iOS's tracked uppercase captions ("SESSION TOPIC", "YOUR MESSAGE") become sentence-case `bodySmall` labels in `onSurfaceMuted` (no letter spacing, no all-caps).

| Role | Size | Weight | Line height | Used for here |
|------|------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Body | 16sp | 400 | 24sp (1.5) | Card info rows (date, time, topic), banner and dialog messages, reason row labels, comment text and placeholder, empty and error body, refund line, unselected tab label, Join card text |
| Label | 12sp | 600 | 16sp (1.33) | `BookingStatusBadge` and status pill text (`labelMedium`). Regular 12sp captions (section labels, "Expert timezone", ID, comment counter, "Refunds typically appear...") use `bodySmall` |
| Heading | 20sp | 600 | 24sp (1.2) | Topic on Upcoming cards and Upcoming Details, expert name on Upcoming Details and Summary, "Confirm Cancellation?" card heading, TopAppBar title, empty and error headings, dialog titles |
| Display | 28sp | 600 | 34sp (1.2) | "Why are you cancelling?" (`headlineLarge`) only |

`titleMedium` (16sp / 600): expert name on list cards, reschedule card header and time values, status labels, selected tab label, the fee value, summary row values. `labelLarge` (16sp / 600): every button label and dialog button. `titleLarge` (20sp / 600): the TopAppBar title, the topic on Upcoming cards and Upcoming Details, dialog titles, empty-state headings.

## Color

Dark only. All colors come from the Phase 1 ColorScheme and `MaterialTheme.extras`. No hex literal appears in this phase's code (`scripts/check-design-rules.sh`). Tinted fills are `color.copy(alpha = ...)` of a scheme color.

| Role | Value | Usage in this phase |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| Dominant (60%) | `#131313` `surface` | All screen backgrounds, app bars, tab row container |
| Secondary (30%) | `#1C1B1B` `surfaceContainerLow` and `#201F1F` `surfaceContainer` | List cards, Upcoming Details cards, Summary card, reason card, Confirm card: `surfaceContainerLow`, `medium`, 1dp `outlineVariant`. Comment field fill and the Cancel summary inner card: `surfaceContainer`. Dialogs: `surfaceContainerHigh` |
| Accent (10%) | `#44F697` `primary` | See reserved list |
| Warning / pending | `#FFB833` `tertiary` | "Waiting for expert" banner, reschedule card (fill 10% alpha, 1dp border 45% alpha), reschedule badge on list cards (same tint), "Missed" badge, "Pending approval" and "New time proposed" status dots and text, "Card hold placed" pill, the warning icon on Confirm, the "no refund" icon. Text on tinted surfaces stays `onSurface`; only icons, dots and short badge labels take the tertiary color |
| Destructive | `#FFB4AB` `colorScheme.error` | The Cancel Booking button (text, icon, 1dp border at 50% alpha), Confirm Cancellation (same), Decline (same), the "Decline" and "Remove"-style confirm text in dialogs, the "Cancelled" badge. Nothing else. (`extras.destructive` `#FF4D4D` fails 4.5:1 on `surfaceContainerHigh`, Phase 7 rule A5, so `error` is used.) Never a filled error button |

Accent reserved for (and nothing else in this phase):
1. Fill of the single primary CTA on each surface: "Explore Experts" (Upcoming empty state), "Retry" (error states, via `EmptyStateAction`), "Accept" (reschedule card), "Continue" (Cancel step 1), "Keep Booking" (Cancel step 2; the safe action is the filled one), "Book Again" (Summary). The disabled Join Call placeholder uses the disabled container, not the accent.
2. The `PrimaryTabRow` selected indicator (3dp) and the selected tab label stays `onSurface` (not accent).
3. Selected `RadioButton` on the reason rows.
4. The "Completed" `BookingStatusBadge`, the "Confirmed" and "In progress" status pill dot, and the "Paid" payment pill.
5. The word "Free" wherever the session fee is shown (Phase 5 D-21).
6. Focused border and cursor of the comment and decline-reason fields, and every `CircularProgressIndicator` (including the pull-to-refresh indicator).
7. The "Accept" text button in the accept-confirm dialog (the single primary action of that dialog).

Neutral, deliberately not accent: the fee amount (`onSurface`, iOS uses green), the tab labels, the card info icons (`onSurfaceMuted`), the "Refunded" pill (`onSurfaceVariant`), the copy-link icon (`onSurfaceVariant`), links, today's-sessions card tap (no visual change beyond the ripple).

Contrast: `onPrimary` on `primary` 9.3:1 (buttons); `tertiary` on `surfaceContainerLow` about 9.5:1 (icons, dots, badge labels); `error` on `surfaceContainerLow` above 8:1; `onSurfaceMuted` on `surfaceContainerLow` at least 5.9:1. Disabled content (Join placeholder) is exempt but stays legible. Status is never conveyed by color alone: every badge, pill and banner carries a word, and the pill/banner carry an icon.

## Motion

Phase 1 durations. All of it follows the system animator duration scale.

| Interaction | Duration / easing |
|---|---|
| Push / pop of Upcoming Details, Cancel, Summary | Phase 1: horizontal slide + fade, 300 ms (`Motion.PUSH_POP_DURATION_MS`, `pushPopEasing`); predictive back drives pops |
| Tab switch (Upcoming / Past) | 150 ms cross-fade of the list (`Motion.CONTROL_STATE_DURATION_MS`); the indicator uses the `PrimaryTabRow` default |
| Cancel step 1 to step 2 and back | `AnimatedContent`: the entering step slides in from the end and fades, the leaving step slides out to the start and fades, 300 ms (iOS .28 s); reversed when going back |
| Button press | Phase 1 press scale 0.96, 120 ms (`PressScaleButton`) |
| Radio selection | 150 ms color change (`animateColorAsState`) |
| Loading to content, silent reload swapping rows | 150 ms cross-fade; rows use `LazyColumn` `animateItem()` defaults when a booking moves tabs after a reload |
| Reschedule card appearing or disappearing after a reload | `AnimatedVisibility` fade and expand, 200 ms (`Motion.STRENGTH_METER_VISIBILITY_MS`) |
| Copy-link icon swap (copy to check) | 150 ms cross-fade, reverts after 2 s |
| Snackbar | Material default |

## Navigation and Structure

```
Client shell (HomeClientScreen entryProvider):
  UpcomingBookingKey(bookingId)   BottomBar.hidden()
  CancelBookingKey(bookingId)     BottomBar.hidden()
  BookingSummaryKey(bookingId)    BottomBar.hidden()
  (keys are @Serializable NavKey data classes; registered next to PaymentMethodsKey)

Bookings tab back stack (ClientBookingsRoot):
  My Bookings (tab root, bottom bar visible)
   |- Upcoming card tap  >  UpcomingBookingKey
   |     |- Cancel Booking  >  CancelBookingKey
   |     |- Reschedule card "Add a Payment Method"  >  PaymentMethodsKey (Phase 7)
   |- Past card tap  >  BookingSummaryKey
         |- Book Again  >  BookingKey (Phase 6, expert seed)
         |- Reschedule card (same as Details)

Explore tab back stack (ExploreRoot):
  Today's Sessions card tap  >  UpcomingBookingKey  (then Cancel, Payment Methods as above, inside the Explore stack)
```

| Screen | App bar | Back behavior |
|---|---|---|
| My Bookings | `TopAppBar`, title "My Bookings" (Android string; the tab label stays "Bookings"), no back, no actions, Phase 1 tab-root colors | Selects the home tab (Phase 1 rule) |
| Upcoming Booking Details | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Upcoming Booking", back arrow ("Back") | Pops. While an accept or decline call is in flight Back still pops; the call finishes in the ViewModel and its result is dropped |
| Cancel Booking | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Cancel Booking", back arrow | Step 2: returns to step 1 (D-08). Step 1: pops. While the cancel call is in flight Back (system and arrow) is ignored and the arrow is disabled (`backEnabled = false`) |
| Booking Summary | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Session Summary", back arrow | Pops |

**After a successful cancel (D-07).** Select the Bookings tab, reset its stack to My Bookings, select the Past tab, refresh (use the existing `resetAndSelect(ClientBookingsRoot, ...)` pattern). It works from either origin (Bookings tab or Explore stack). My Bookings then shows a `Snackbar` with the result copy; the tab selection and the pending snackbar message travel as launch arguments (for example a one-shot field on the shared client state), not as a nav result, so both origins behave the same.

**Back from Payment Methods to Upcoming Details (D-02).** The details screen reloads quietly on resume. The client taps Accept again; nothing continues automatically.

**Today's Sessions hand-off (surface 6).** Each `TodaysSessionCard` becomes one `Role.Button` click target (`onClickLabel` "View booking details") that navigates to `UpcomingBookingKey(session.bookingId)` in the Explore stack. The card keeps the inner disabled Join Call button, its test tag `todays_session_join` and its state description; the card is not merged into one semantics node, so TalkBack still reads the disabled Join state. Visual change: none beyond the ripple.

Insets: the tab root content handles the bottom bar via the shell. Pushed screens use `safeDrawing`; scroll content uses `contentPadding` with `xl` bottom plus the navigation-bar inset. The Cancel comment field uses `imePadding` and keeps the field in view when focused.

## Screen Specifications

### 1. My Bookings (BOOK-05, D-09 to D-12)

Background `surface`. Layout, top to bottom: app bar, tab row, content.

**Tab row.** `PrimaryTabRow` with two `Tab`s, "Upcoming" and "Past" (iOS order, default Upcoming), container `surface`, no divider line other than the default, indicator `primary` 3dp, each tab `heightIn(min = 48.dp)`. Selected label `titleMedium` `onSurface`, unselected `bodyLarge` `onSurfaceVariant` (no weight jump beyond the two allowed weights). `md` horizontal padding is not applied to the row (the row is full width, each tab half). The selected tab is `rememberSaveable`. Switching tabs does not clear the other tab's list. Semantics: `Role.Tab`, selected state.

**Content.** Each tab has its own list state. `lg` top padding under the tab row, `md` horizontal padding, `md` between cards, `xl` plus nav-inset bottom. The list is wrapped in `PullToRefreshBox` (D-10); the empty and error states are inside a `verticalScroll` `fillMaxSize` column so pull-to-refresh also works from them.

| State | Rendering |
|---|---|
| Loading (first load of a tab) | `SkeletonGroup` with three skeleton cards copying the real card shape: Upcoming skeleton = 56dp circle plus two text bars (160 x 20 and 120 x 16), `md`, a 200 x 16 bar, `md`, a full-width 56dp bar; Past skeleton = a 160 x 20 bar plus a 80 x 24 badge bar on one row, `sm`, a 200 x 16 bar and a 160 x 12 bar. `md` between cards. Group `contentDescription` "Loading" |
| Failed | `EmptyState` (`Outlined.ErrorOutline`): heading "Couldn't load bookings", body = the server message, else "Check your connection and try again.", action "Retry" (filled; re-runs the load with the Loading state). `liveRegion = Polite` |
| Empty, Upcoming | `EmptyState` (`Outlined.CalendarMonth`): heading "No Bookings Yet!", body "Start your learning journey by finding an expert that suits your needs.", action "Explore Experts" (filled; selects the Explore tab, resets its stack to the root) |
| Empty, Past (D-11) | `EmptyState` (`Outlined.History`): heading "No past bookings yet", body "Completed, cancelled and missed sessions will show up here.", no action |
| Loaded | Cards as below |
| Quiet reload (resume, tab re-shown, `booking.update`, pull-to-refresh result) | The list stays on screen and updates in place. Never returns to the Loading state. A failed quiet reload keeps the previous list and shows nothing (the pull-to-refresh indicator just ends). A failed pull-to-refresh shows a `Snackbar` "Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again." (Android) |

The first load of a tab happens the first time the screen is composed; the other tab loads when it is first selected. The rejoin rule, sorting, labels and unknown-status handling are CONTEXT logic and not visual contract.

**Upcoming card (`UpcomingBookingCard`).** `surfaceContainerLow`, `medium`, 1dp `outlineVariant`, `md` padding. The whole card is one `Role.Button` click target (ripple, `onClickLabel` "View booking details"); the Join placeholder is disabled, so it does not intercept taps. Layout, top to bottom, `md` between blocks:

| Block | Spec |
|---|---|
| Expert row | `Avatar` 56dp, `md`, text column. Line 1: expert name `bodyLarge` `onSurfaceVariant`, `maxLines = 1`. Line 2 (`xs` below): topic `titleLarge` `onSurface`, `maxLines = 2`, ellipsis. Fallbacks reuse `explore_expert_name_fallback` and `explore_session_topic_fallback` |
| Date and time | One `Row` that wraps (`FlowRow`, `md` horizontal gap, `sm` vertical gap): `Outlined.CalendarMonth` 20dp `onSurfaceMuted`, `sm`, date (`formatShortDate`, `bodyLarge` `onSurface`); then `Outlined.Schedule` 20dp, `sm`, time range (`formatSessionTimeRange`, `bodyLarge` `onSurface`). Device time zone and device 12/24 hour setting |
| Reschedule badge (only when `reschedule != null`) | Tinted row (`tertiary` 10% fill, 1dp `tertiary` 45% border, `small`), `md` horizontal and `sm` vertical padding, `Outlined.Update` 16dp `tertiary`, `sm`, text `labelMedium` `onSurface`. Copy by initiator: expert: "Expert proposed a new time · Tap to review"; client: "Reschedule pending expert's response". Text wraps to 2 lines |
| Pending-approval banner (status `pending_approval`; replaces Join) | Tinted row as above, `Outlined.HourglassEmpty` 16dp `tertiary`, "Waiting for expert to accept" `labelMedium` `onSurface` |
| Join placeholder (every other status; D-12) | `PressScaleButton(onClick = {}, enabled = false)` full width, `heightIn(min = 56.dp)`, `Outlined.Videocam` 20dp, `sm`, "Join Call" (`explore_join_call`), `stateDescription` "Available closer to the session start" (`explore_join_call_state`), test tag `my_bookings_join`. `// PHASE-9` marker as in Today's Sessions. A status the backend counts joinable but that is `pending_reschedule` shows Join the same way |

Reschedule is hidden for good (DEF-1), Message is hidden until Phase 12, Private Note is hidden (D-16). A card with a reschedule badge and `pending_approval` cannot occur together; if it does, the badge shows above the banner.

**Past card (`PastBookingCard`).** Same container as the Upcoming card, `md` padding, the whole card is one `Role.Button` target ("View summary") that opens Booking Summary. Layout:

| Block | Spec |
|---|---|
| Header row | `FlowRow`: expert name `titleMedium` `onSurface` (weight 1, wraps, `maxLines = 2`), `BookingStatusBadge` at the end (wraps below the name at large font scales) |
| Topic | `sm` below: "Topic: {topic}" as `bodyLarge`; the label "Topic:" in `onSurfaceVariant`, the value in `onSurface` (one `Text` with two spans, no weight change) |

No Leave Review button and no private note preview (Phase 13, D-16).

**`BookingStatusBadge`** (shared). `CircleShape`, fill = color at 12% alpha, 1dp border = color at 60% alpha, text `labelMedium` in the color, `sm` horizontal and `xs` vertical padding (content-driven height, not a touch target). Values:

|---|---|---|
| Completed | `completed` | `primary` |
| Cancelled | `cancelled`, `declined` | `error` |
| Missed | `missed`, `expired` | `tertiary` |

### 2. Upcoming Booking Details (BOOK-06, D-01, D-03, D-12, D-14, D-15)

Background `surface`. Scroll column, `md` horizontal padding, `md` top padding, `md` between blocks, `xl` bottom.

| State | Rendering |
|---|---|

| Failed | `EmptyState` (`Outlined.ErrorOutline`): heading "Couldn't load booking", body = server message, else "Check your connection and try again.", action "Retry" |

   | `in_progress` | In progress | `primary` |
   | `completed` (rejoin window) | Completed | `primary` |

## Interaction Contract (summary)

| Rule | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Refresh | My Bookings: pull-to-refresh, a quiet reload when the screen or tab is shown again, and a quiet reload on `booking.update` from the Phase 3 socket (D-10). Upcoming Details: quiet reload on resume and on `booking.update` for its id. Only the first load of a tab or screen shows a skeleton |
| One response at a time | While accept or decline is in flight, both reschedule buttons and Cancel Booking are disabled; a second tap does nothing (iOS `isSubmittingReschedule`) |
| After a reschedule response | Reload the booking (D-02..D-04). Success shows a `Snackbar` "Reschedule accepted" or "Reschedule declined" (Android); the card disappears with the 200 ms fade |
| Not optimistic | Nothing flips before the server answers. The card, status pill and times change only after the reload |
| Cancel flow | Two steps, Back from step 2 returns to step 1 (D-08). In flight: Back disabled, taps ignored. Success: result snackbar on My Bookings Past tab (D-07). The step, reason and comment survive rotation and process death |
| Join | Inert everywhere in this phase. Disabled placeholder with the Today's Sessions state description |
| Tab selection | Saved across rotation (`rememberSaveable`). Returning to the tab root keeps the selected sub-tab; a successful cancel selects Past |
| Rotation / process death | Entry-scoped Hilt ViewModels with `SavedStateHandle` (Phase 4/6 pattern): booking id, selected sub-tab, cancel step, reason, comment, dialog flags and the decline text are restored. In-flight network calls complete in the ViewModel |
| Realtime | Raw status strings are kept (D-10); unknown statuses are dropped from lists and shown as a neutral pill on details |
| Money | Minor units everywhere except the cancel response `refund_amount` (major units); display only via `Money.format` (JPY no decimals) |

## Accessibility Contract

- Touch targets at least 48dp: tabs, cards (whole card), copy button, dialog buttons, Retry. Controls are 56dp.
- Each Upcoming and Past card is one merged `Role.Button` node. Upcoming reads "{expert}, {topic}, {date}, {time range}", then the banner or badge text if any, then "Join Call, available closer to the session start" for the disabled placeholder. Past reads "{expert}, {topic}, {date}, {time range}, {Completed|Cancelled|Missed}".
- Tab row: `Role.Tab`, selected state announced. Pull-to-refresh is also reachable by TalkBack's "Refresh" custom action on the list (`PullToRefreshBox` provides it).
- Status pills, badges, payment pills and banners carry a word, not color alone; icons are decorative (`contentDescription = null`).
- Headings (`heading()`): reschedule card title, Cancel headline, Confirm heading.
- Reason rows: `selectableGroup` with `Role.RadioButton`, the label read once ("Change of plans, selected").
- In-flight buttons expose `stateDescription` ("Accepting", "Declining", "Cancelling"). Loading and error states use `liveRegion = Polite`; dialogs are announced by the platform.
- Cancel step change: after Continue, focus moves to the step 2 heading; after Back from step 2, to the step 1 headline.
- Copy-link success is announced by the icon `contentDescription` swapping to "Copied".
- 200% font scale: cards, rows, badges, pills and banners wrap (`FlowRow` for the header rows), the reschedule buttons stack, reason labels wrap and the row grows (`heightIn`, never fixed), the Confirm card and dialogs scroll. Check My Bookings, Upcoming Details with a reschedule card, Cancel step 2 and Summary.
- A full TalkBack pass is Phase 15 (POL-04).
