<objective>
Write the pure, Android-free availability logic with JVM unit tests first: the schedule model, hydration, overlap check, edit operations, the HH:mm wire format, picker option lists, display formatting, the SavedStateHandle codec, the day-name join and the timezone rules.

Purpose: EXP-03 and EXP-04 behaviour lives here so the ViewModel (10-12) and the components (10-08) stay thin. The traps (24:00, `LocalTime.of(24, 0)` throwing, locale digits, DST-gap labels, off-grid values from other clients) are covered by tests.
Output: two Kotlin objects and two test classes.

Assumption recorded (research open question 1, resolved with the research recommendation): the End picker keeps the iOS 23:30 cap, but the model, hydration and request builder round-trip 1440 minutes ("24:00") losslessly, the picker always includes a slot's current off-grid or 24:00 value, and nothing calls `LocalTime.of(24, 0)`. Offering midnight as a selectable End is left for the user to approve later (logged by the gate plan).
</objective>
