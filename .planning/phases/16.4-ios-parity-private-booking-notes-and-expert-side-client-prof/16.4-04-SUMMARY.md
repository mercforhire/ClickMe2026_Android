# Phase 16.4 Plan 04: Client Profile data and state Summary

**Assisted Hilt ExpertClientProfileViewModel that loads a client's profile then both bookings buckets into a filtered, newest-first history, with a status-gated net-earnings amount and distinct NoRelationship, Unavailable, Failed and pull-to-refresh states**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 13 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-02T02:54:03Z
- **Completed:** 2026-11-02T03:07Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 7 (5 created, 2 modified)

## Accomplishments
- Tracer proven through the real repositories over the fake API: profile first, then `upcoming` and `past` on page 1 with limit 50, merged, filtered with a case-insensitive id compare, deduplicated, newest first.
- `clientHistoryBucket` ports iOS `mapStatus`; an unknown status is Upcoming. `ClientHistoryRow.amountText` gates on status, so cancelled, declined, missed and expired rows show no amount even though the server's breakdown is populated there.
- `netEarningsText` now lives in `ExpertDisplay.kt` and is shared by the Upcoming card and the history; `ExpertBookingsScreenTest` still passes unchanged.
- Failure states: only the profile call can yield `NoRelationship` (403 `NO_RELATIONSHIP`) or `Unavailable` (404), and neither fetches bookings. Any other profile failure or either bookings failure is the whole-screen `Failed`; a failed pull over loaded content keeps it and raises `refreshFailed`.

## Deliberate iOS deviations
- **D-09:** iOS always shows "$0" because its amount is a stub; Android shows the real net earnings, and nothing when the net amount or currency is missing or the row is in the cancelled bucket.
- **D-14:** iOS shows a generic error with Retry for a 403 `NO_RELATIONSHIP`; Android shows a dedicated NoRelationship state with no Retry.
- **D-15:** iOS shows a generic error with Retry for a 404; Android shows a dedicated Unavailable state with no Retry.

## Flagged assumptions (planned to the recommended default, NOT confirmed by the user)
- **A1:** an unknown booking status code maps to the Upcoming bucket with no amount.
- **A6:** no amount on any `missed` row, even client-no-show rows that were paid, because the list payload has no `no_show_party`. A late client cancel on a captured booking is paid too and also shows no amount (RESEARCH Open Question 1).
