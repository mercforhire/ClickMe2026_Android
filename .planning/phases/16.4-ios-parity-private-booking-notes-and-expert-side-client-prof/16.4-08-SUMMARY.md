# Phase 16.4 Plan 08: Client Profile screen Summary

**Read-only expert-side Client Profile in Compose: static-glow avatar, displaySmall name with Client pill, About, Your Sessions Together and a booking history with real net earnings, over skeleton, ErrorState, specific 403 and 404 states and the refresh-failed snackbar**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 35 min (start time was not recorded at launch; estimated from the session)
- **Completed:** 2026-11-02T03:34Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 9 (all created)

## Accomplishments

- Tracer: a loaded profile renders under the "Client Profile" bar from the real `ExpertClientProfileViewModel` over the fake API, with a Hilt route (`ExpertClientProfileRoute(key, onBack)`) ready for plan 16.4-09.
- The full iOS layout in the iOS order with the iOS copy: About (bio card plus Location, Timezone, Languages, Member since, each hidden when empty), Your Sessions Together (plural count, `MMM yyyy` dates, a dash for null) and the Booking History.
- History rows are plain read-only surfaces: no click action, no role. They show the net earnings from `ClientHistoryRow.amountText(locale)`, nothing on free or cancelled rows.
- Every load state from plan 16.4-04 renders: skeleton, `ErrorState` with Retry, the NoRelationship and Unavailable states with "Go back" and no Retry, and a failed pull over loaded content keeps it and raises the snackbar. Every state scrolls, so pull-to-refresh works everywhere.

## Decisions Made

- The Client pill is read with the name through one merged node (spec: "read with the name").
- The Failed state is vertically centered; the two friendly states are top-aligned with an `xxxl` offset, matching how the spec lays out each.
- `ClientProfileCard`, `ClientProfileSectionHeading` are `internal` in `ClientProfilePersonalInfo.kt` rather than a new file, to keep the file list to the plan's.

## Deliberate iOS deviations (repeat for the phase record)

- **D-09:** real net earnings on history rows instead of the iOS stub "$0"; nothing when free or in the cancelled bucket.
- **D-14:** a 403 `NO_RELATIONSHIP` shows "No bookings with this person yet" with "Go back" and no Retry (iOS: generic error with Retry).
- **D-15:** a 404 shows "Profile unavailable" with "Go back" and no Retry.
- **Phase 15 loading states:** skeleton, `ErrorState` and the refresh-failed snackbar replace the iOS centered spinner.
