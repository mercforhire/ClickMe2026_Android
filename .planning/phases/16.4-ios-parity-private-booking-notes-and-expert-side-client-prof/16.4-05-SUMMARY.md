# Phase 16.4 Plan 05: Private note on the My Bookings cards Summary

**Client My Bookings cards now carry the private note: "Private Note" plus a preview footer on every past card, a note zone as the last child of every upcoming card, fed by a new `private_note_preview` DTO field and routed to `ClientBookingNoteKey` in the shell.**

## Accomplishments

- Tracer (Task 1): `ClientBookingItem.privateNotePreview` (redacted `toString`), `PastBookingCard.onPrivateNote` with `PrivateNoteAction` sharing a `FlowRow` with the review pill, `NotePreviewFooter` for a non-blank preview, a second TalkBack custom action, `MyBookingsScreen`/`MyBookingsContent`/`TabBody` threading, and `onPrivateNote = { navigator.navigate(it) }` on `bookingsRoot`. The tracer verify (`MyBookingsScreenTest` + `ktlintCheck`) passed end to end before expansion.
- Task 2: `UpcomingBookingCard.onPrivateNote` and `PrivateNoteZone` after every action branch (confirmed with and without Reschedule, pending approval); Join, Message and Reschedule layouts untouched.
- Task 3: tests only. Decoding (preview, absent, explicit null, redacted `toString`) and `aSavedNoteShowsOnReturn*` for both lists, proving D-08 with no new refresh code.
- Wider check: `screens.clientmode.*` and `navigation.*` unit tests plus `ktlintCheck` pass.

## Deliberate iOS deviations

- D-08: iOS can show a stale preview on return; Android relies on the existing quiet reload on `onScreenShown`, tested rather than re-implemented.
- A blank (whitespace-only) preview counts as absent on both cards, stricter than the iOS `isEmpty`.

## Note for plan 16.4-06

The `clientBookingNoteEntry` registration lands in 16.4-06. Until then, tapping a note control navigates to a key with no entry. This only matters between the two plans inside one phase run.
