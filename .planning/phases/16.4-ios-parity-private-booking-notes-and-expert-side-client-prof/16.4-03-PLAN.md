<objective>
Build the one shared note editor both roles use: load, edit, Save with spinner and saved state, the discard gate, the 5,000 cap and the whitespace rule, plus the two thin Hilt routes that bind a key to a role.

Purpose: this is where every rule in D-01..D-05 lives, so it is one ViewModel with JVM tests rather than two copies (iOS has two identical pairs that differ only in endpoint and brand tokens). The leading tracer proves one role end to end on screen: GET, show, edit, Save, POST.
Output: 3 new files under `screens/bookingnote/`, 2 new route files, the editor strings, 2 new test classes.

Deliberate iOS deviations implemented here (repeat them in the SUMMARY): D-01 (iOS silently discards), D-02 (iOS has no cap, so an over-long note only fails on save), D-04 (iOS saves whitespace as typed), and Phase 15 loading states (skeleton and `ErrorState` replace the iOS centered spinner and error view).
</objective>
