## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] PrivateNoteAction target narrower than 48 dp**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** The wrap-content "Private Note" row only had a height floor; `assertTouchTargets` found it 40 px wide (Robolectric's legacy text measurement makes the label very narrow, but a short label could produce a sub-48 dp target on a device too).
- **Fix:** `defaultMinSize(minWidth = 48.dp, minHeight = 48.dp)` instead of `heightIn(min = 48.dp)`.
- **Files modified:** `components/booknote/PrivateNoteParts.kt`
- **Verification:** `PrivateNotePartsTest` 10/10 pass.
- **Committed in:** `a09d957c`

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] KDoc containing a slash-star sequence broke the Kotlin and ktlint parser**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The planned KDoc wording for `expertClientProfileEntry` included the path glob for the expert endpoints, whose slash-star opened a nested comment and ktlint failed to parse the file.
- **Fix:** Reworded to "the `/expert` path prefix".
- **Files modified:** `screens/expertmode/clientprofile/ExpertClientProfileEntries.kt`
- **Committed in:** `b67894c8`
