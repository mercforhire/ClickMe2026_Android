<objective>
Wire the two Payment Methods entry points and the return path: register `PaymentMethodsKey` in the client shell, add the Profile row, route the "Add a Payment Method" CTA to Payment Methods, and refresh the saved cards when the booking form comes back.

Purpose: D-04 (two entry points, one destination), D-08 (the gate opens Payment Methods), D-09 (reload on return, re-tap to continue). RESEARCH A10 is uncertain whether a Nav3 entry gets ON_RESUME when the entry above it pops, so the refresh is driven both by ON_RESUME and by the entry re-entering composition, and the re-entry is verified in a Robolectric nav test.
Output: screen routing, shell registration, Profile row, nav and screen tests.
</objective>
