<objective>
Bring the Stripe Android SDK into `:app` and build the three Stripe-side seams every later plan uses: app-start `PaymentConfiguration`, one shared sheet configuration plus dark appearance, and an injectable `pm_` reader.

Purpose: RESEARCH Pitfall 1 found that `:app` has no Stripe dependency yet (only `:spikes:stripe` uses it), and the phase gate includes R8 `assembleProdRelease`. Every Stripe UI (add-card PaymentSheet, booking FlowController, 3DS PaymentLauncher) needs the dependency, the init and the same configuration. Keeping the SDK behind small seams keeps the ViewModels pure and unit-testable (RESEARCH Pattern 1).
Output: the dependency line, the init, `stripe/StripePaymentMethodReader.kt`, `stripe/ClickMePaymentSheetStyle.kt`, `di/StripeModule.kt`, a test fake and a configuration/appearance test.
</objective>
