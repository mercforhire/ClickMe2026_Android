<objective>
Make SC-4 flow 3 (join a call) pass the TalkBack contract, prove that recreation and restoration never auto-join, and keep the call controls reachable on short large-screen windows.

Purpose: POL-02, POL-03, POL-04; CONTEXT D-02, D-11, D-16 tier 1. Most call semantics already ship (status live region, heading, Role.Switch states, End state); the gaps are the verified order, focus on entry, the pill-first rule (RESEARCH: `traversalIndex` is used 0 times) and the short-window layout.
Output: small semantics and layout changes, a flow test and restoration tests.
</objective>
