<objective>
Make a process kill during a live call land the user back on that booking's detail screen with Join Call and a one-line note, never rejoining by itself; and bring the two booking detail screens up to the D-05 refresh standard.

Purpose: POL-01, POL-02; CONTEXT D-04, D-05, D-11. RESEARCH Pattern 5 found no persisted in-call marker: after a process kill the restored back stack shows whatever screen was under the call layer (often a list), which does not meet D-11's "lands on the booking/session detail screen". This plan adds the smallest marker that does, reusing the Phase 13 push routing (which already resolves a booking to Upcoming Details or the expert Summary) instead of a new router.
Output: a DataStore-backed marker, a memory notice, app-start wiring, the note on both detail screens, their D-05 refresh path and tests.
</objective>
