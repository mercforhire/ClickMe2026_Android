<objective>
Make a process kill during a live call land the user back on that booking's detail screen with Join Call, never rejoining by itself, and flag the one-time resume note for that booking.

Purpose: POL-02; CONTEXT D-11. RESEARCH Pattern 5 found no persisted in-call marker: after a process kill the restored back stack shows whatever screen was under the call layer (often a list), which does not meet D-11's "lands on the booking/session detail screen". This plan adds the smallest marker that does, reusing the Phase 13 push routing (which already resolves a booking to Upcoming Details or the expert Summary) instead of a new router. The note on the two detail screens and their D-05 refresh path are 15-18 (wave 2), split out per the plan checker so the marker, DI and app-start wiring ship as one reviewable unit.
Output: a DataStore-backed marker, a memory notice, app-start wiring and the marker tests.
</objective>
