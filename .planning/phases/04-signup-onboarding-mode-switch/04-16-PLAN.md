## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-41 | Elevation of privilege | Picker URI | mitigate | Read once by PhotoProcessor.importPicked; never stored in state, saved state or nav keys (acceptance grep: no SavedStateHandle in this ViewModel) |
| T-04-42 | Denial of service | Upload spam | mitigate | One in-flight upload (Saving guard); the server's upload limiter (20 per 15 min) returns 429, shown in the dialog with no retry loop |
| T-04-43 | Tampering | Wrong part type rejected by multer | mitigate | The repository builds the part as `image` with `image/jpeg` (plan 04-07, tested) |
| T-04-44 | Information disclosure | Photo files left in cache | mitigate | clearWorkingFiles after a successful upload and on remove of an unsaved pick; logout and publish clear them through the repository |
</threat_model>
