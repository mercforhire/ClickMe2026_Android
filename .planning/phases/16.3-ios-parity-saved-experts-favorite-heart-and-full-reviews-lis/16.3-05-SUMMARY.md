# Phase 16.3 Plan 05: Full Reviews screen Summary

Full Reviews screen (iOS `ExpertReviewsView`): "{First}'s Reviews" app bar, overall rating card with a half-star row and pluralized count, review cards with avatar ring, topic, date label and a 2-line clamped comment, plus skeleton, error with Retry, inline empty state and pull-to-refresh with the refresh-failed snackbar.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer):** `strings_expert_reviews.xml` with every string for the screen, `ExpertReviewsCard`, and `ExpertReviewsScreen` / `ExpertReviewsContent` over the real `ExpertReviewsViewModel` and the fake API. The app bar title is a heading, one line with an ellipsis, and falls back to "Reviews" for a blank name. Back arrow calls `onBack`.
- **Task 2:** `starKinds` (Full, Half, Empty per the iOS rule) and `ExpertReviewsStarRow` using `Icons.AutoMirrored.Filled.StarHalf`; `ExpertReviewsOverallCard` (rating to one decimal, "1 Total Review" singular, one merged TalkBack sentence "Overall rating 4.5 out of 5, 12 Total Reviews"). The overall card is the first lazy item.
- **Task 3:** `PullToRefreshBox` over every state with a 300 ms `Crossfade`; skeleton of one 112 dp and three 120 dp blocks in one "Loading" group; `ErrorState` "Couldn't load reviews" with the server message and Retry; zero reviews show the overall card then "No reviews yet" and its body; `RefreshFailedSnackbarEffect` keeps content on a failed pull. Each review card is one TalkBack node joining name, "Rated N out of 5", topic, date label and the FULL comment.
