## Findings

### W1: Overall card is centered, not baseline-aligned (real deviation, minor)

- UI-SPEC: "a baseline-aligned row". iOS: `HStack(alignment: .firstTextBaseline)`.
- `ExpertReviewsOverallCard.kt:58` uses `verticalAlignment = Alignment.CenterVertically`.
- Effect: the 28sp rating sits on the vertical midpoint of the stars and count column.
- Suggested fix: align by `FirstBaseline`, then check on a device. Baseline alignment against a column whose first child is a star row needs care, because the icons have no text baseline.

### W2: No rendered verification

Unchecked on a device: the half-star row, the 144 dp skeleton against the real card height, the `FavoritesGlowingStar` animation, dialog styling, and the profile heart cross-fade. These stay open under the deferred manual testing items.

### Noted, not deviations

- Reviews stars use `tertiary` where iOS draws them green. The spec documents this.
- The overall rating is 28sp against iOS 48sp. The spec declares this, but the focal point may read weaker than on iOS, so look at it during the device pass.
- The Saved Experts heart is 48 dp against iOS 36 dp. The spec requires this for the Android touch target. A long two-line name at large font scale loses 36 dp of text width and needs a device check.

## Top fixes

1. Restore baseline alignment in `ExpertReviewsOverallCard.kt` (W1).
2. Device pass on the Saved Experts card with long names at large font scale.
3. Check on a device that the 28sp overall rating still reads as the focal point.
