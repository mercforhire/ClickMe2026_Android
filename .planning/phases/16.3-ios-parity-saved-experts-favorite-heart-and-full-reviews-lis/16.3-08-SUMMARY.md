## Decisions Made

- Reset in the callbacks, not in the shared sets: adding the Profile tab to `showMyBookings` / `showExplore` would break D1.FV4 and reset the stack for `MyBookingsScreen.onExploreExperts` and cancel `onCancelled`.
- Membership of this `RequestSentKey` in the Profile stack decides the reset, so an Expert Profile left open on the Profile tab survives a booking made elsewhere.
