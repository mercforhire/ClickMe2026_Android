<objective>
Let any user delete their account from inside the app and land on Login after a farewell, exactly as iOS does, without the session teardown hiding the farewell.

Purpose: PROF-04 is a Play Store requirement and is never cut. The flow ports iOS `DeleteAccountView`: Warning, then Reason with the password, one confirm that runs request-deletion and the final DELETE (D-05, UI-SPEC A1), then the Farewell. Deleting revokes every session server-side, so any later authenticated call can force a logout and swap the route; the Farewell is therefore hosted at the root, outside the logged-in shell (RESEARCH Pitfall 3).
Output: key, reason enum, repository, state singleton, view model, two-step screen, Farewell host wired into `ClickMeApp`, one new error code, strings and three test classes. 14-14 registers `DeleteAccountKey` and links it from Account Security.
</objective>
