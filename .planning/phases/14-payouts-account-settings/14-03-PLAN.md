<objective>
Build the client and expert profile hubs as stand-alone screens with navigation callbacks, plus the self-view flag on the public Expert Profile.

Purpose: PROF-01. The hubs replace the throwaway Profile footers (CONTEXT D-01..D-04) and show only rows that work. They take callbacks instead of keys, so they build now in Wave 1 while the destinations are built in parallel; 14-14 plugs them into the shells and supplies the keys. Self-view (D-02, RESEARCH Pitfall 8) lets an expert open their own public profile without a Book Session control that would end in `422 CANNOT_BOOK_SELF`.
Output: two hub screens with view models, the shared hub components, a strings file, the self-view flag and four test classes.
</objective>
