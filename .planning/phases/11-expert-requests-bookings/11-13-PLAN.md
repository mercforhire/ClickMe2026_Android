<objective>
Add the reschedule response to the expert Booking Summary end to end: the expert error mapper, the expert responder behind the shared controller, the guarded accept and decline in the ViewModel, and the expert variant of the shared card with its dialogs, snackbars and errors.

Purpose: EXP-09 "accept or decline a client's reschedule request (handling 409 SLOT_UNAVAILABLE)". The client mapper is client-worded and has no SLOT_UNAVAILABLE branch, so the expert gets its own (C9); the client responder retries with a saved card, so the expert gets its own responder (RESEARCH Pattern 5). `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE` comes from the database exclusion constraint (30-minute session buffer, SESSION-BUFFER.md).
Output: mapper, responder, ViewModel and screen changes, and three test classes.
</objective>
