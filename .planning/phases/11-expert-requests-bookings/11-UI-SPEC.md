# Phase 11 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
>
> **Scope.** Requirements EXP-05 to EXP-09. Seven surfaces:
> 1. The expert Dashboard (`DashboardRoot` tab root): hero, pending and reschedule banners, Today's Sessions with Join, financial summary with the payout onboarding card, the Expert Tools grid.
> 2. Incoming Requests (new route): list, empty, loading, error, expired state.
> 3. Request Decision (new route) with the Accept sheet and the Decline sheet.
> 4. Booking Confirmed (new route; replaces Request Decision after an accept) with "Add to Calendar".
> 5. Expert bookings (`ExpertBookingsRoot` tab root): Upcoming and Past tabs, session and past cards, Join.
> 6. Expert Booking Summary (new route): details, Join, the expert variant of `RescheduleResponseCard`, Cancel entry.
> 7. Expert Cancellation (new route): two steps, refund preview for the client.
>
> Out of scope and must not appear in the UI: Message / Message User / Message Client buttons (Phase 12), starting a reschedule (DEF-1), the private note card, "Private Note" and "Add private note" buttons (Phase 8 D-16), "View Profile" on the client header (DEF-3), the Payouts screen, Profile Settings and the `clickme://connect/*` return screens (Phase 14), paging past page 1, the iOS "Accepted!" / "Declined" footer states (D-03, D-04), the "Pricing mismatch" decline reason (D-01).
>
> **Baseline.** `01-UI-SPEC.md` (tokens, shapes, motion, shell), `06-UI-SPEC.md` (Request Sent confirmation pattern), `08-UI-SPEC.md` (booking cards, status badge and pill, tinted rows, two-step cancel, reschedule card, `AuthErrorDialog`, result snackbars, destructive rule A5), `09-UI-SPEC.md` (`JoinCallButton`, "Starting now" badge, guard dialog) and `10-UI-SPEC.md` (expert tab roots, tool-icon tiles, `AvailabilityKey`) are locked and not restated. Dark only, Material 3, Nunito 400 / 600, exactly four text sizes (12 / 16 / 20 / 28 sp), `Spacing` xs..xxxl, `ClickMeShapes`, `MaterialTheme.extras`. Every value below is an existing token unless marked "(new)".
>
> **Design source.** Google Stitch is not accessible. The stand-in is `ios-reference/ClickMe2026/Views/Screens/Expert Mode/**` (Expert Dashboard, Incoming Requests, Request decision, Confirmed, Upcoming Bookings, Booking Summary, Cancellation). Copy is taken from iOS verbatim unless marked "(Android)". The per-screen iOS palettes (`IncomingRequestsTheme`, `RequestDecisionTheme`, `ExpertCancellationBrand`) collapse to the Phase 1 tokens (FND-02); iOS hard-coded amber, red and glow shadows become `tertiary`, `error` and no glow. Layout is ported and adapted to Material 3, not pixel-matched.
>
> **Source of decisions.** CONTEXT D-01..D-18 and its "ported as-is" blocks for behavior and pinned copy. This spec fills the "Claude's Discretion" areas: layout, spacing, the look of every card, banner, badge and sheet, the extra copy for dialogs, snackbars and states, and the "coming soon" placeholder (a snackbar). The run had no live questions, so every assumption is recorded in "Assumptions and Open Items". No RESEARCH.md exists; the money-unit and endpoint items listed in CONTEXT "Research items" are carried as open items and do not change the visuals.

## Spacing Scale

Phase 1 tokens, all multiples of 4: xs 4, sm 8, md 16, lg 24, xl 32, xxl 48, xxxl 64.

| Token | Used for in this phase |
|-------|------------------------|
| xs (4dp) | Gap between a name line and its sub-line; label to value; icon-to-text gap inside badges and pills; badge vertical padding; gap between the "Earnings" caption and the amount |
| sm (8dp) | Icon-to-text gap in info rows; gap between rows inside a card; badge horizontal padding; gap between a card header and its first row; gap between stacked dialog and sheet fields; gap between Dashboard section heading and its content |
| md (16dp) | Screen horizontal padding (iOS 16 to 20); gap between cards in every list and column; card inner padding; gap between avatar and text; gap between the two footer buttons and between sheet buttons; sheet horizontal padding; gap between tool cells |
| lg (24dp) | Gap between Dashboard sections (iOS 24) and between Request Decision blocks; top padding under the app bar and under the tab row; gap between the check badge, headline, card and buttons on Confirmed; sheet bottom padding above the navigation inset; inner padding of the Confirm-cancellation card |
| xl (32dp) | Bottom scroll padding on every screen plus the navigation-bar inset (iOS 40 to 48); `EmptyState` icon-to-heading gap (inherited) |
| xxl (48dp) | Minimum touch target: tabs, text actions, icon buttons, tool cells' inner icon tile, decline reason rows are 56 |
| xxxl (64dp) | Not used as spacing |

Exceptions (component dimensions and platform minimums, not layout spacing):
- Controls (Join Call, Accept, Decline, Continue, Keep Booking, Confirm Cancellation, Add to Calendar, Back to Home, sheet buttons) use `heightIn(min = 56.dp)`.
- Avatars: 56dp on list cards (Incoming Requests, Upcoming, Past 48dp), 48dp on Today's Sessions cards and the Past card and the Cancel summary, 72dp on the Request Decision client header, 64dp on the Booking Summary header, 40dp on the Confirmed details card.
- Icon tiles: 48dp tile (`small` shape, `primary` 12% fill) with a 24dp glyph on the pending banner, the onboarding card and the Expert Tools cells. Info-row icons 20dp; pill and banner icons 16dp or 24dp as stated.
- Expert Tools cell: `heightIn(min = 112.dp)` (iOS 120), two cells per row, `md` gap both ways.
- Confirmed: check badge 128dp circle, check glyph 64dp (Phase 6 values). Warning circle on Cancel step 2: 64dp, glyph 32dp.
- Avatar ring on Incoming Request cards: 2dp stroke around the 56dp avatar (drawn outside the avatar bounds, so the touch target is unchanged).
- Request Decision footer bar: `md` padding plus the navigation inset around two 56dp buttons.
- Comment and message fields: `heightIn(min = 112.dp)`. Stroke widths 1dp resting, 2dp focused. Skeleton shapes copy the real dimensions above.

## Navigation and Structure

```
Expert shell (HomeExpertShell), per-tab Navigation 3 back stacks. Detail entries use BottomBar.hidden().

Dashboard tab stack:   DashboardRoot (tab root, bottom bar visible)
   |- pending banner / "Pending Requests" tool   >  IncomingRequestsKey
   |      |- request card tap  >  RequestDecisionKey(requestId)
   |             |- Accept success  >  BookingConfirmedKey   (REPLACES RequestDecisionKey)
   |             |- Decline success >  pop to IncomingRequestsKey (refreshed) + snackbar
   |- Today's Sessions card tap  >  ExpertBookingSummaryKey(bookingId, Dashboard)
   |- "Availability" tool        >  AvailabilityKey(AvailabilityOrigin.Dashboard)
   |- "View Calendar", reschedule banner, "Calendar" tool  >  select Bookings tab (Upcoming)
   |- "Topics" tool              >  select Topics tab

Bookings tab stack:    ExpertBookingsRoot (tab root)
   |- any card tap  >  ExpertBookingSummaryKey(bookingId, Bookings)
          |- Cancel Booking  >  ExpertCancelKey(bookingId)

Sheets and dialogs (not routes): Accept sheet, Decline sheet, accept / decline reschedule dialogs
```

Keys are `@Serializable NavKey` data classes registered next to `AvailabilityKey` in the expert shell. Keys that can appear in two tab stacks (`ExpertBookingSummaryKey`, `ExpertCancelKey`) carry an `origin` value, as `AvailabilityKey` does, so entry-scoped ViewModels stay distinct per stack. Incoming Requests and Request Decision are pushed on whichever tab stack started them (Dashboard in practice).

| Screen | App bar | Back behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Tab-root `TopAppBar`, title "Dashboard", avatar action (existing `TopBarAvatarButton`), no back | Phase 1 rule |
| Incoming Requests | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Incoming Requests", back arrow | Pops to the Dashboard |
| Request Decision | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Booking Details", back arrow | Pops to Incoming Requests. While Accept or Decline is in flight, Back (arrow, system, predictive) and sheet dismissal are ignored (`backEnabled = false`, Phase 8 convention) because the result decides where the user goes |
| Booking Confirmed | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Booking Confirmed", back arrow | Pops to Incoming Requests, which reloads quietly (D-04). Never returns to a decision screen |
| Expert Bookings | Tab-root `TopAppBar`, title "Bookings", no back | Selects the home tab (Phase 1 rule) |
| Expert Booking Summary | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Booking Summary", back arrow | Pops. While a reschedule response is in flight Back still pops; the call finishes in the ViewModel and its result is dropped |
| Expert Cancellation | `AuthTopAppBar`, title "Cancel Booking", back arrow | Step 2: returns to step 1. Step 1: pops. In flight: ignored and the arrow is disabled |

**Back to Home (D-05).** Resets the Dashboard stack to `DashboardRoot`, selects the Dashboard tab if another tab is selected, and triggers a quiet reload so the pending count drops. Works from any origin.

**After a successful decline (D-03) / accept (D-04).** One-shot launch arguments (not nav results) on the shared expert state carry the snackbar message and the "reload on arrival" flag, so Incoming Requests behaves the same however it is reached.

**After a successful cancel (D-17).** Select the Bookings tab, reset its stack to `ExpertBookingsRoot`, select the Past sub-tab (Android; mirrors Phase 8 D-07 so the cancelled booking is visible), reload, and show the snackbar. Works from either origin.

**Tab switches** from the Dashboard (Calendar, Topics, reschedule banner, "View Calendar") use the shell navigator's select-tab call; the Dashboard stack is left as it is. Bookings opened by the reschedule banner and "View Calendar" land on Upcoming.

Insets: tab-root content handles the bottom bar via the shell. Pushed screens use `safeDrawing`; scroll content uses `contentPadding` with `xl` bottom plus the navigation-bar inset. Sheet fields use `imePadding()`.

## Screen Specifications

### 1. Dashboard (EXP-05, D-09 to D-13)

Background `surface`. `PullToRefreshBox` around a `verticalScroll` column: `md` horizontal padding, `lg` top, `lg` between sections, `xl` plus navigation inset at the bottom. Refresh: pull-to-refresh, a quiet reload when the tab is shown again (and on `Lifecycle.ON_RESUME`, which also re-fetches Connect status, D-09), and a quiet reload on a realtime `booking.update` (D-13).

| State | Rendering |
|---|---|
| Loading (first load) | `DashboardSkeleton`: a 160 x 28 bar (hero), a 72dp banner block, a 24dp heading bar plus two 128dp card blocks, a 24dp heading bar plus a 128dp card block, then a 24dp heading bar plus three rows of two 112dp blocks. `md` between. Group `contentDescription` "Loading" |
| Hard failure (bookings or requests failed) | `EmptyState` (`Outlined.ErrorOutline`): heading "Couldn't load dashboard", body = server message, else "Check your connection and try again.", action "Retry" (filled). `liveRegion = Polite`. Inside a `verticalScroll` `fillMaxSize` column so pull-to-refresh works |
| Loaded | Blocks below, in order. The financial fetches (weekly earnings, payout summary, Connect status) are soft: a failure never changes this state |
| Quiet reload | The content stays on screen and updates in place; never returns to the skeleton. A failed pull-to-refresh shows a snackbar "Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again."; a failed resume or realtime reload shows nothing |

Blocks, top to bottom:

1. **Hero.** Caption "Dashboard overview" (`bodySmall`, `onSurfaceMuted`), `xs`, then "Hello, {first name}" (`headlineLarge`, `onSurface`, wraps, `heading()`). First name = first word of the profile name; blank name shows "Hello, there".
2. **Pending requests banner (`PendingRequestsBanner`).** `surfaceContainerLow`, `medium`, 1dp `outlineVariant`, `md` padding, whole card is one `Role.Button` (`onClickLabel` "Review requests"). `Row`: 48dp icon tile (`primary` 12% fill, `NotificationsNone` 24dp `primary`), `md`, column (title `titleMedium` `onSurface`: plural "{n} Pending Requests" / "1 Pending Request"; subtitle `bodySmall` `onSurfaceMuted` "Review and confirm new bookings"), `ChevronRight` 24dp `onSurfaceVariant`. The count is `pagination.total_count`, falling back to the item count. Zero state (Android): title "No pending requests", subtitle "New bookings will show up here"; the card stays tappable and opens the empty Incoming Requests list. Test tag `dashboard_pending_banner`.
3. **Reschedule banner (`RescheduleRequestsBanner`, only when the client-initiated count is above zero).** `tertiary` 10% fill, 1dp `tertiary` 45% border, `medium`, `md` padding, one `Role.Button` (`onClickLabel` "Review reschedule requests"). Same row layout: 48dp tile (`tertiary` 12% fill, `Update` 24dp `tertiary`), title `titleMedium` `onSurface` plural "{n} Reschedule Requests" / "1 Reschedule Request", subtitle `bodySmall` `onSurfaceVariant` "Review pending time changes", chevron. Selects the Bookings tab on Upcoming (D-12). Appears and disappears with the 200 ms fade and expand. Test tag `dashboard_reschedule_banner`.

## Accessibility Contract

- Touch targets at least 48dp: tabs, banners, cards (whole card), tool cells, text actions, copy button, dialog and sheet buttons. Controls are 56dp. Decline reason rows are 56dp.
- Cards expose one merged `Role.Button` node: request card "{client}, {topic}, {date}, {time range}, expires in {n} hours" or "..., expired"; session card "{client}, {topic}, {time range}" plus "Starting now" when shown, with the Join button as its own focus stop; Upcoming card "{client}, {topic}, {date}, {time range}" plus the badge text; Past card "..., {Completed|Cancelled|Missed}".
- Banners and tool cells are `Role.Button` with an `onClickLabel`. The earnings block has one description "{net} this week · {n} sessions".
- Status, badges, pills and banners carry a word; icons are decorative (`contentDescription = null`). An expired request is identified by the word "Expired" and not only by the amber ring.
- Headings (`heading()`): "Hello, {name}", section headings, sheet titles, "This request has expired", the Cancel headline and Confirm heading, "Booking Confirmed!".
- Reason rows use `selectableGroup` with `Role.RadioButton`, read once ("Schedule conflict, selected"). The required marker on "Reason for declining" is announced.
- In-flight buttons expose `stateDescription` ("Accepting", "Declining", "Cancelling", "Opening secure setup"). Loading and error states use `liveRegion = Polite`; "Booking Confirmed!" is a polite live region.
- Disabled "Decline Request", "Continue" and "Join Call" explain themselves through `stateDescription`.
- Sheets: TalkBack focus lands on the title; the IME never covers the focused field (`imePadding()`).
- Cancel step change: focus moves to the new step's heading.
- 200% font scale: cards, banners, badges, header rows and the Join/earnings rows wrap (`FlowRow`); the footer and reschedule buttons stack; tool cells grow (`heightIn`, never fixed) and labels wrap to 2 lines; the Decline sheet, Confirm card, dialogs and Confirmed scroll. Check the Dashboard, a request card, Request Decision with the earnings card and footer, the Decline sheet, Confirmed, an Upcoming card with the reschedule badge and Cancel step 2.
- Reduce motion: with the animator scale at 0 every transition and the Confirmed entry are instant.
- A full TalkBack pass is Phase 15 (POL-04).

## Assumptions and Open Items (for the planner and checker)

The run was non-interactive, so these were decided without the user:

- **A1. Zero pending state on the Dashboard banner** shows "No pending requests" / "New bookings will show up here" and stays tappable. iOS shows "0 Pending Requests". Revert to the iOS wording if preferred.
- **A2. "Coming soon" placeholder is a snackbar** ("{tool} is coming soon"), not a screen (CONTEXT left it to discretion). Phase 14 swaps the handler.
- **A3. `AvailabilityOrigin.Bookings`** is a new origin value for the Upcoming empty state's "Update Availability" (CONTEXT only names `Dashboard`). If the planner prefers not to add it, drop that action and keep "View Past History".
- **A4. Earnings card is always shown** on the Dashboard with the onboarding card added below when required, because EXP-05 lists weekly earnings and the onboarding card together; iOS shows one or the other.
- **A5. Expired request cards are not dimmed.** The badge, the amber ring and the "Expired" word carry the state.
- **A6. Incoming Request card earnings** show "Payout pending" when the list item has no net amount; the exact list payload is a research item.
- **A7. Decline sheet uses radio rows** instead of an iOS-style dropdown, with nothing preselected and "Decline Request" disabled until a reason is chosen.
- **A8. "Keep Request" is the filled primary** on the Decline sheet (the safe action is accent, mirroring Phase 8 "Keep Booking").
- **A9. Accept sheet confirm label** is "Accept Request" (verb + noun) instead of iOS "Confirm"; the title stays "Confirm Booking".
- **A10. Expert Cancel has no preselected reason** and Continue is disabled until one is chosen (iOS "Select a reason..."). The Phase 8 client flow preselects "Change of plans".
- **A11. After a cancel the Past sub-tab is selected**, mirroring Phase 8 D-07, so the cancelled booking is visible.
- **A12. Confirmed does not fetch.** It receives the data from the decision screen (no extra request). If the accept response is richer than expected, nothing changes visually.
- **A13. Tab titles:** the expert bookings app bar says "Bookings" (iOS "Upcoming Sessions"), the Dashboard app bar "Dashboard".
- **A14. Call-guard action names** (`SetUpPayouts`, `RespondToReschedule`) and their strings are Android text; the planner may rename them.
- **A15. Past card earnings** are not shown (iOS Past card has none); only the Upcoming card shows the net amount.

Open items:

1. Research items from CONTEXT stay open and do not change the visuals: weekly earnings units and null `net_amount` (surface 1), the exact Connect endpoint response shapes, and whether `GET /expert/bookings/:id` exposes `payment_status` and `amount_paid` for the refund preview (surface 7; if not, the refund row falls back to "Any refund follows our cancellation policy.").
2. iOS follow-ups to log (from CONTEXT): decline codes and `reason_text`; the orphaned Confirmed screen; the calendar title using the expert's name; no Join on the Summary; "full refund" always promised on an expert cancel. New from this spec: the expired card dimming fails contrast.
3. Backend-owner note: an expert cancel within 24 hours currently gives the client no refund (D-16).
4. Manual checks deferred (project preference): accept and decline against an iOS client, the Confirmed screen and calendar insert on the Samsung SM-A075F, expired request, payout onboarding Custom Tab, Join from the three sites with the iOS client, reschedule accept/decline and `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE`, expert cancel, 200% font scale on the screens listed above.
5. Phase 12 will add the Message buttons; Phase 14 replaces the Payouts and Profile Settings placeholders and the balance card tap.
