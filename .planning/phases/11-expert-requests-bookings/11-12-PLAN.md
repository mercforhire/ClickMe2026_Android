<objective>
Add the decision actions to Request Decision end to end: the pinned footer, the Accept sheet and the Decline sheet, the ViewModel's single-flight accept and decline with the backend's strict bodies, the error mapping with the `ALREADY_PROCESSED` exit, and the navigation outcomes (Confirmed replaces the screen; decline pops with a snackbar).

Purpose: completes EXP-06. Uses the backend's four decline codes and the required `reason_text` with a label fallback, fixing two iOS defects that make iOS decline fail with 422 (D-01, D-02; iOS follow-ups recorded by 11-15). Builds on 11-06's read side, 11-05's hand-off consumer and 11-07's Confirmed destination.
Output: two logic files, ViewModel and screen changes, three sheet/footer composables, registration update, strings and three test classes.

Backend follow-up (record, do not change behavior): `POST expert/booking-requests/:id/accept` validates `message` (max 1000) but never reads it (RESEARCH C10, Open Question Resolution 2). The message is still sent for iOS parity and future-proofing; 11-15 logs the note in STATE.
</objective>
