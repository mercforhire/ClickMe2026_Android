<objective>
Close G-16.2-1 (major) in code: the client's Reschedule button is hidden only while a reschedule is pending, on Upcoming Booking Details and on My Bookings upcoming cards, as on iOS.

Purpose: UAT Test 1 found no Reschedule button on either client surface. Diagnosis (`.planning/debug/client-reschedule-button-missing.md`) showed no rendering, wiring or data defect: the D-04 gate hid the button for bookings under 24 h away and for pending approval. The pending-approval card also needs a layout change, because its branch never rendered Reschedule even when the caller offered it.

Output: a status-only rule, both call sites updated, the pending-approval card arrangement, and the tests that encoded the old rule rewritten to the new one. Docs (CONTEXT D-04 amendment, UI-SPEC, matrix row) and the newly reachable call-guard path (IN-01) are plan 16.2-14. </objective>
