# Phase 16.2 Plan 10: Expert Reschedule entry on Expert Booking Summary

**Expert Booking Summary now offers Reschedule exactly when the server's `actions.can_reschedule` is true, at the start of a row with Cancel Booking at the end, opens the expert picker with the summary's origin and the `ExpertSummary` opener, and on return reloads quietly and shows "Reschedule request sent" once.**

## Accomplishments

- **Entry point (Task 1, tracer):** `ExpertSummaryDetails.canReschedule = actions.canReschedule`; the screen renders `ActionButtonPair(start = Reschedule when canReschedule, end = Cancel when canCancel)`. One alone fills the width, neither leaves no row. No client 24 h or awaiting-approval rule applies (a `pending_approval` booking 30 minutes out still shows it). Reschedule is disabled while an accept or decline is in flight. `expertBookingSummaryEntries` pushes `ExpertRescheduleKey(key.bookingId, key.origin, RescheduleOpener.ExpertSummary)`.
- **Return path (Task 2):** `ExpertBookingSummaryViewModel` takes `RescheduleSentRequest`, consumes the `ExpertSummary` slot for its booking in `init` and while alive, raises `rescheduleSent` once and, when covered, reloads quietly so "Awaiting client's response" shows. The content shows `reschedule_start_sent` through the composition scope and consumes the flag. Another booking's notice and the `ExpertBookings` slot are left alone.
