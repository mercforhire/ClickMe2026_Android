# Phase 16.2 Plan 06: Client picker state holder and ViewModel Summary

**A shared `ReschedulePickerController` plus the client Hilt `RescheduleViewModel` and `RescheduleKey`: the picker loads the booking, opens on its month and day, loads three months excluding the booking, applies the pickability rules, guards and discloses before a single-flight send, maps every outcome to its dialog and after-OK action, and hands success to exactly one origin slot.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto TDD)
- **Files created:** 4 (3 main, 1 test class with 34 tests)
- **Verification run:** `RescheduleViewModelTest` 34/34; whole `screens.clientmode.reschedule.*` package 122 tests, 0 failures; `ktlintCheck` clean

## Accomplishments

- Load and seed: booking detail then calendar seeded from the booking's start in the device zone (later of that day and today); centre month and both neighbours load in parallel with `exclude_booking_id = bookingId`; a neighbour failure is silent, a centre failure is `AvailabilityState.Failed`; stale responses are dropped.
- Selection: `selectSlot` goes through `isPickable` (paid cap now + 6 days inclusive, current slot, past, held); changing day clears the slot; month moves keep the date; `refreshClock` clears a started slot but never re-checks the paid cap and never sends.
- Submit: no slot or current slot refuse locally with no request; the call guard (`RescheduleBooking`) runs next; the D-11 disclosure opens before any request; Cancel makes no request and keeps date, slot and message; Continue sends once.
- Outcomes: `Sent` posts `RescheduleSent(bookingId)` to the opener's slot and sets the one-shot `sent`; `NeedsPaymentMethod`, `CardUnusable` and `CardsUnavailable` map to their dialogs; every `ProposeFailure.Show` clears the slot, reloads the months, or sets `leave` after OK. After Payment Methods nothing is sent until the next Submit tap.
- Process death: month, date, slot start, message and the disclosure flag are saved and restored; a restored slot is re-validated after the first availability load; the disclosure is re-opened with `chargesSoon` recomputed. `isSubmitting`, dialogs other than the disclosure flag, and any card id are never saved.

## Decisions Made

See key-decisions. The plan said a month move "keeps the selected date and slot" and also that Submit stays disabled when the date's month is outside the loaded window. `ReschedulePickerUiState.canSubmit` only looks at the slot, so the slot is cleared when the date leaves the window; within the window it is kept and re-validated when the months arrive.
