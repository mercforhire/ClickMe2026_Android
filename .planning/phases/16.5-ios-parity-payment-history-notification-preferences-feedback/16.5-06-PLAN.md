<objective>
Build notification preferences' state and rules with no UI: `AutoSaver`'s debounce parameter, the 13-row grid, the repository, and the ViewModel that hydrates without saving and saves without losing edits.

Purpose: PARITY-AUDIT #5's behavior, with D-01's correction applied. `AutoSaver` already solves ordering, retry and flush, so the risk is in the seams: a debounce that defaults to the wrong value, and a hydrate that would PATCH every row on open as iOS does. The leading tracer goes from a load through one flip to one PATCH.
Output: 1 modified main file, 3 new main files, 1 new strings file, 1 fixture file, 1 new test class, 1 extended test class.
</objective>
