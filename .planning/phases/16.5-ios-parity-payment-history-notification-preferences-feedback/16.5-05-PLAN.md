<objective>
Build Payment History's state and rules with no UI: the repository, the paged ViewModel with its 500-past-the-end rule, and the pure status and amount logic.

Purpose: PARITY-AUDIT #4's behavior is in these rules, and each has a specific way to go wrong silently (telling a declined client that nothing happened, showing `$0.00` for a row with no amount, an error at the end of every list). They are unit-tested here before the screen exists. The leading tracer goes from the fake API through the real repository to the first page in state.
Output: 3 new main files, 1 new strings file, 1 fixture file, 2 new test classes.
</objective>
