<objective>
Build the wire contract the rest of Phase 16.5 compiles against: five Retrofit calls, their DTOs, the `FakeClickMeApi` overrides, and MockWebServer tests that pin the exact method, path, body and null handling.

Purpose: three parts of this contract are easy to get wrong and silent when wrong: `amount` null versus 0, the raw `payment_status` vocabulary, and the strict PATCH schema that rejects any extra key with a 422. All three are pinned here, before any screen depends on them. The leading tracer proves one call end to end (URL constant, Retrofit method, DTO, fake override, wire test).
Output: 7 main files (5 new), 3 new test classes, 2 extended test classes.
</objective>
