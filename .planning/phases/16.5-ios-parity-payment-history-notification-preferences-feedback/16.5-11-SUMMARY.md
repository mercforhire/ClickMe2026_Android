# Phase 16.5 Plan 11: Hub wiring Summary

Both profile hubs now reach Payment History (client only), Notifications (new Preferences section) and Send Feedback, in iOS order, with every route hiding the bottom bar and popping back to the hub.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer):** Payment History row after Payment in the client Account section, mapped to `ClientTransactionsKey`; the entry passes `navigator::openUpcomingBookingFromProfile` so a row opens Upcoming Booking on the Profile stack (D-03). Shell tests drive hub -> list -> booking, the two-step Back chain, and the same booking open on Explore and through Payment History (no Nav3 content-key collision, T-16.5-28).
- **Task 2:** Preferences section (Notifications) between Account and Support, Send Feedback between FAQ & Help and Privacy on the client hub; `NotificationSettingsKey` and `FeedbackKey` entries (hidden bar, `hiltViewModel()`) in the shared `accountAndHelpEntries`. Hub tests cover order, callbacks, the absence list, and a 2x font scale at 360 dp (captions and rows stay inside the width, rows at least 56 dp tall).
- **Task 3:** The same Preferences section and Send Feedback row on the expert hub, no Payment History. `SettingsEntriesTest` pins all three keys hidden on the client provider, two on the expert provider, and `expertEntriesDoNotRegisterPaymentHistory` asserts the expert provider rejects `ClientTransactionsKey` (T-16.5-29). `HomeShellsTest` adds `clientHubReachesNotificationsAndFeedback` and `expertHubReachesNotificationsAndFeedback`.
