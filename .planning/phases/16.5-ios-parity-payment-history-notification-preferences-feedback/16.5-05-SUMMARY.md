# Phase 16.5 Plan 05: Payment History state and rules Summary

Payment History's repository, paged ViewModel (500-past-the-end rule, keep-rows refresh, booking-id dedupe) and pure status, amount, refund and date logic, all unit-tested with no UI.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer):** `ClientTransactionsRepository` over `getClientTransactions`, `ClientTransactionsViewModel` loading page 1 through the real repository and the `FakeClickMeApi`, plus the shared `clientTransaction` / `clientTransactionsPage` fixtures. A page-1 500 is `Failed`, not an empty list; duplicate booking ids in a page are dropped.
- **Task 2:** `refresh`, `consumeRefreshFailure`, `onRowVisible` (prefetch within the last 3 rows, one request at a time, `has_more` only), `retryPage`, `loadNextPage` and `appendNew`. A 500 on page 2 or later sets `hasMore = false` and no `pageError`; any other failure keeps the rows and shows the Retry footer. A refresh cancels the page in flight, and `retryPage` during a refresh is ignored.
- **Task 3:** `ClientPaymentStatus` (13 entries, own vocabulary), tints, `clientPaymentStatusOf` (`none` with an amount above 0 is Processed, "cancelled" is Unknown), `isPartialRefund`, `clientAmountText` (JPY none, CAD two, null has no text), `clientRowDate`, `amountIsMuted`, and `strings_payment_history.xml`.
