<objective>
Build Send Feedback's state and rules with no UI: the repository, the 10-character gate, the email prefill, the error mapping, and the sent state.

Purpose: PARITY-AUDIT #6 with D-05's stricter validation and D-06's thanks dialog. The backend rejects short details and a blank or invalid email, so the client's job is to send a request that can succeed and map a failure to the right place. The leading tracer goes from the types load through a valid submit to the sent state.
Output: 3 new main files, 1 new strings file, 1 fixture file, 1 new test class.
</objective>
