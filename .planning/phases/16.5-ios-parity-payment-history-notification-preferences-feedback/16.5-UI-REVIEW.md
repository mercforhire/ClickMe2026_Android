## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Unchecked switch uses `outline` for thumb and border** (`NotificationSettingsScreen.kt:313-314`) — the contract names `surfaceContainerHigh` track and `onSurfaceVariant` icon for the unchecked state, and does not list `outline`. Low impact, but it is an undeclared color role. Fix: align it to the contract or add the slot to the spec.
2. **Raw `.dp` literals in screen files** (`ClientTransactionsScreen.kt:264` `2.dp`, `NotificationSettingsScreen.kt:210` `spacedBy(1.dp)`, `SplashScreen.kt:62,85,152`) — the splash values are spec-declared (520, -20, 148), but the 1dp subtitle gap contradicts the declared `xs` (4dp) "notification subtitle gap". Fix: use `Spacing.xs`, or confirm 1dp against iOS and amend the spec.
3. **Nothing was visually verified** — splash glow and pulse, pill tints at 12% alpha, the Notifications app-bar status states and the Feedback Details field error border need a device pass (Phase 16 matrix). Fix: add the three screens and the splash to the on-device matrix.

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (4/4)
- Greps of `strings_payment_history.xml`, `strings_feedback.xml`, `strings_splash.xml`, `strings_notification_settings.xml` and `strings_profile_hub.xml` match the UI-SPEC copy table.
  - Covered: the 12 status labels, the empty state, error titles, the four notification section headings, all 13 row labels, "In-app, Email, Push", and the changed Feedback strings (10-character validation, "Thanks for your feedback!" with OK).
  - Also covered: the hub rows "Payment History", "Notifications", "Send Feedback" and the "Preferences" caption.
- No "Submit", "Click Here" or "Something went wrong" generic labels. The CTA is "Submit Feedback" / "Feedback Sent!".
- Minor: `payment_history_row_a11y` strings are `translatable="false"`. They are format-only, but a11y status words are interpolated in; harmless while English-only.
- The "Before you go" card and quiet hours are intentionally absent (D-10, D-01). Grep for "Before you go" found nothing in `app/src/main`, as the contract requires.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (3/4)
- Payment History row (`ClientTransactionRow.kt`): left column holds topic, expert and date, optional refund line, then pill. Amount sits on the right. This follows the EarningsTransactionRow anatomy. The pill is non-interactive and uses text, so status is never color-only.
- Notifications: whole-row `toggleable(role = Role.Switch)`, section headings carry `heading()` semantics, and the status action sits in the app bar.
- Splash: a single merged semantic node. The mark is decorative (`contentDescription = null`). The glow is a radial gradient drawn from the `primary` slot with no hex.
- Not verified: how the glow reads against the radial background, and whether the pulse is perceptible. Static review cannot judge focal-point strength.
- WARNING: no screenshots exist for any new surface.

### Pillar 3: Color (3/4)
- No `Color(` literals in the six audited screen files. `scripts/check-design-rules.sh` reports "Design rules: OK".
- Accent use in `NotificationSettingsScreen.kt` is limited to the checked track, saving spinner, saved check and Retry text (lines 310, 347, 355, 378), as the contract reserves.
- Pill tints use `primary`, `tertiary` and `error` at `PILL_FILL_ALPHA` with `onSurface` text, as specified.
- WARNING: `uncheckedThumbColor` and `uncheckedBorderColor` use `outline` (lines 313-314), which is not in the declared slot list.
- Not verified: the 60/30/10 split on rendered output. Notifications is mostly green when most toggles default on, which the spec accepts.

### Pillar 4: Typography (4/4)
- Audited files use only `MaterialTheme.typography.*` styles: `titleMedium`, `bodySmall`, `labelMedium`, `bodyLarge`, `displaySmall`.
- No `fontSize` or `FontWeight` references in the six files. The splash wordmark uses `displaySmall` (28sp) and the tagline `bodyLarge` (16sp), as the spec's deviation note says.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)
- `Spacing.md/xs/sm/xl` are used for row padding, column gaps, pill padding and the splash gaps.
- Declared fixed sizes are present: 180dp Details minimum, 148dp mark, 190/160dp glow, 520dp gradient radius, 56dp notification row, 48dp status slot, and skeleton sizes.
- WARNING: `spacedBy(1.dp)` at `NotificationSettingsScreen.kt:210` is off the 4dp scale and conflicts with the spec's `xs` for the subtitle gap.
- Minor: `strokeWidth = 2.dp` is a component dimension and acceptable.
- Not verified: bottom inset handling (`xl` plus navigation-bar inset) beyond the design-rules script.

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)
- State coverage: Payment History has skeleton, empty, error, refresh and next-page footer states. Notifications has skeleton, error and save status. Feedback has skeleton, error, a type picker and thanks/error dialogs.
- Accessibility is handled well: merged row nodes, `Role.Switch`, `heading()`, live-region status, and `AnimationsEnabled` gating for the splash.
- The design-rules script passes, covering Scaffold padding and IME handling.
- WARNING: all of this is code-derived. Splash motion with animations off, auto-save retry, and 422/429 Feedback paths are covered only by unit tests, not on-device.
- No destructive actions exist in this phase. "View Past History" is already shipped and was not re-audited.
