Recipe reference (RESEARCH Pattern 1, Apache-2.0, github.com/android/nav3-recipes multiplestacks): NavigationState holds startRoute, a saveable topLevelRoute and one NavBackStack per top-level route (rememberNavBackStack); stacksInUse = [startRoute] when topLevelRoute == startRoute else [startRoute, topLevelRoute]; entries are decorated with rememberSaveableStateHolderNavEntryDecorator + rememberViewModelStoreNavEntryDecorator and flattened over stacksInUse. Navigator.navigate(route) selects a top-level route or pushes onto the current stack; goBack() at a tab root selects startRoute, otherwise removes the last entry.
