<objective>
Build the root-level call dialogs and the microphone permission requester as stateless composables plus pure decision functions.

Purpose: `CallCenter` queues typed `CallDialog` values and publishes a pending mic request; the overlay host (09-13) renders the queue head with `CallDialogHost` and fulfils the pending request with `MicPermissionRequester`. Keeping the decision table pure makes CALL-02 testable without a device.
Output: `CallDialogs.kt`, `MicPermissionRequester.kt`, three test classes.
</objective>
