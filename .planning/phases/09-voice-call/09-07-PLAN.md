<objective>
Absorb the spike's `CallService` findings into `:app` as a thin microphone foreground service plus a singleton bridge (the production `CallForegroundController`) and an isolated notification factory, and declare the permissions, the service and `singleTop` on `MainActivity`.

Purpose: CALL-03 — the in-app call keeps running with audio focus and a persistent notification while backgrounded, screen-off and after a task swipe (D-14). The service holds no call logic: `CallCenter` starts it (after the mic grant, before the engine) and stops it after `/end`; the notification's End call goes back to `CallCenter` through the bridge (D-10).
Output: `CallService.kt`, `CallForegroundBridge.kt`, `CallNotificationFactory.kt`, manifest edits, `ic_stat_call.xml`, channel creation in `ClickMeApplication`, three Robolectric test classes.
</objective>
