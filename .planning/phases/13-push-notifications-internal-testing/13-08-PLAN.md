<objective>
Build everything behind the notifications list except the pixels: grouping by local day, relative time labels, the `**bold**` body parser, the row-to-destination mapping, and a ViewModel with first load, quiet reloads (resume and foreground-push tick), pull-to-refresh and row taps that feed the push pipeline.

All of it is pure Kotlin or a plain ViewModel, so the date edges (midnight, DST) and the parser are JVM-tested with `MutableClock`. 13-12 renders it. If the schedule slips, this plan and 13-12 are the first cut (CONTEXT carried cut order).
Output: row model, grouping, parser, UI state, ViewModel, four test classes.
</objective>
