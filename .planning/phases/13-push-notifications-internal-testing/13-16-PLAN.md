<objective>
Ask for `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` exactly when iOS asks for push permission (right after login or signup), exactly once per install, with no rationale screen, and never over the login form or a live call.

Purpose: PUSH-02's permission half and D-01 / D-02. The "asked" fact must survive logout (unlike everything in the session store), and the trigger must not fire on session restore. A pure decision table keeps the rules JVM-testable; the composable mirrors Phase 9's `MicPermissionRequester`.
Output: a `sessionStarts` counter on UserManager, the asked flag in `PushPreferences`, `PushPermissionGate`, the requester and its ViewModel, the `ClickMeApp` mount, four test classes.
</objective>
