<objective>
Wire Firebase Cloud Messaging into the build without making CI depend on the real Firebase file, and give every ClickMe push a channel, a status-bar icon and the brand accent: the guarded google-services plugin, the Firebase BoM, the manifest meta-data FCM uses for system-displayed notifications, and the five notification channels created at app start.

Purpose: PUSH-02 ("notifications post to defined notification channels") and the REL-05 build prerequisite. With this plan, a background push the system displays already lands on the `general` channel with the ClickMe icon; after 13-02 deploys, it lands on the category's channel. Channel ids are a contract with the backend.
Output: Gradle wiring, manifest meta-data, the status icon, the accent color, `NotificationChannels`, channel strings, two test classes.
</objective>
