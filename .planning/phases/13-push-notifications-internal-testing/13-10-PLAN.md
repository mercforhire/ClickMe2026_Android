<objective>
Handle the two ways a push reaches the app: while it is in the foreground (post our own notification on the right channel, tick the list, hide the push for the chat on screen), and when the user taps any ClickMe notification (read the launcher intent's extras on a cold start or `onNewIntent` and hand the destination to the pending slot exactly once).

Purpose: D-12, D-13, D-15 and the tap half of PUSH-04. FCM displays background `notification` messages itself and delivers their `data` as launcher-intent extras with CLEAR_TOP (RESEARCH F-2), so our foreground post uses the same intent shape and both paths meet in `MainActivity`. Routing itself happens in the root host (13-11), never in the service (Anti-Patterns).
Output: `onMessageReceived`, handler, notification factory and poster, MainActivity intake, three test classes.
</objective>
