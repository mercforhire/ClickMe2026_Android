<objective>
Give push routing a way into the two per-tab Navigation 3 shells: a `ShellRoute` hand-off that each shell consumes only for its own mode, one `Navigator.openRouted` method that inserts a key chain into the right tab without duplicate keys, and the `NotificationsKey` / `WriteReviewKey` routes registered in the shells with route bodies that 13-12 and 13-13 fill.

Purpose: PUSH-04's riskiest part is the per-tab back stack, and it is mostly a duplicate-key and ordering problem (RESEARCH Summary). Like 12-03, this is the only Phase 13 plan that edits the shells for these routes, so the feature plans in later waves run in parallel without touching `HomeClientScreen` / `HomeExpertScreen` (13-12 adds the bell callback and 13-15 the review callbacks, each in its own wave).
Output: `ShellRoute` / `ShellRouteRequest` / `ShellRouteViewModel`, `Navigator.openRouted`, `PushRoutingModule`, the two keys with routes and entries, two strings files, three test classes.
</objective>
