<objective>
Mount the root push router: a `PushRoutingViewModel` that watches the app's root route and the pending destination, resolves the tap with 13-07's resolver once a home shell exists, performs a mode switch when the destination belongs to the other mode, and hands the final route to the matching shell (13-04) or to the chat hand-off (Phase 12).

Purpose: the stage-1-to-stage-2 bridge of RESEARCH Pattern 2. The resolver needs suspend lookups, roles and the call guard; the shells are stateless and the target shell only exists after a mode switch, so this host is the single place that waits for splash and restore, drops taps on Login (D-14), and keeps only the newest tap (D-15).
Output: the ViewModel and role seam, the host composable, the `ClickMeApp` mount, two test classes.
</objective>
