Put Write Review where users will find it: restore Booking Summary's Leave a Review button and Feedback block (reversing Phase 8 D-16), add the Leave a review pill to completed Past booking cards, and make both screens reload and thank the user after a submit.

Purpose: REV-01 / ROADMAP success criterion 4 ("a user can write a review from the session/ended and review/nudge pushes and from Booking Summary"). Pushes land on Booking Summary (D-07, 13-07), so the button there completes the push path; the Past card is the D-18 shortcut.
Output: the review state on Booking Summary, the Feedback block and button, the Past card pill, the hand-off consumers, the navigation callbacks, strings and five updated test classes.
</objective>
