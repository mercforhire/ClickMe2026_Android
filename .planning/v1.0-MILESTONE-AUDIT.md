## Cross-Phase Integration

The integration checker traced 16 connections statically. 15 are wired:

- **Phase 9 → 10/11:** `CallGuard` is on availability save, expert reschedule and both cancel paths. `CallStarter.startCall` is called from all 6 Join sites.
- **Phase 9 → 12:** the in-call Chat control goes through `ChatLauncher` to `openChatThread` in both shells. The `PHASE-12` marker is gone.
- **Phase 11 → 12:** Message buttons call `chatEntry.open` from 8 sites.
- **Phase 10 → 11:** the Dashboard pushes `AvailabilityKey(Dashboard)`.
- **Phases 11/12 → 13:** the push resolver builds `RequestDecisionKey`, `ExpertBookingSummaryKey`, `UpcomingBookingKey`/`BookingSummaryKey` and `ChatThreadKey`. The pending slot holds a tap through Splash and onboarding.
- **Phases 2/3 → all:** logout runs 8 `SessionDataCleaner`s and unregisters push. The socket is gated on login plus foreground or an active call, and it reconnects after a token refresh. 6 screens consume `booking.update`. The old "events have no consumer" seam is closed.
- **API:** every `ClickMeApi` method has a caller.

**Partial (non-blocker, PUSH-01):** on forced logout (`SessionEvent.Rejected` or `Suspended`), the token DELETE goes out with no bearer and the server row goes stale. The local `deleteToken()` still invalidates the FCM token. I checked this in `UserManager.kt:131-137` and `FcmPushTokenRegistrar.kt:71-85`.
